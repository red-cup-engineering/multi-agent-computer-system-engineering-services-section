Assistance that follows work through

muse-assist is the 561 Group's early-alpha project for personal, virtual, and executive assistance, designed as a specialized MetroPlat server.

It is being designed to research decisions, coordinate appointments and follow-through, produce usable computer artifacts, and carry authorized work across interruptions. Relevant context, its sources, and your corrections should remain available as that work continues.

An action is only part of the job. The design also calls for checking whether the intended result was received by the person or system that needed it, while keeping consequential actions under attributable human authority.

An open-source project in development

These are design targets. The complete assistant path has not yet been demonstrated, and this site offers neither a hosted assistant nor a supported public package. We are preparing for open-source release; licensing remains undecided.

Read the development method and status and the assistant references and comparison evidence. Meta's Muse is a separate product and a reference for this work. No whole-platform parity or measured advantage is claimed.
