## What MetroPlat's front door is

MetroPlat is the Group's agentic orchestration platform. Its instances are its human front doors:
- https://cold-red-gate.561.group;
- https://bare-cedar-fog.561.group;
- the others.

The Leader (2026-10-02): they need "muuuuuuuch better UX for humans", and "we need to meet then beat everyone for what those websites should be". The Leader (2026-10-03): "I want to focus on MetroPlat as the weirdest wildest coolest UX for the wildest weirdest agentic orchestration platform anyone is making lol, which we are." The Leader again, the same day: MetroPlat should meet and beat other agentic orchestration platforms' UX "by, truly, giving users *and* agents a rich MMORPG/MUD experience."

The front door is a shared world, not a dashboard.
- **Tiers.** It is a rich 3D MMORPG first, with a 2D fallback ("don't be normie: we want rich 3d MMORPG, fallback to 2d"), and text as the MUD tier.
- **One client.** It is the same client as Farstone, on QwzSpace (2026-10-03: "Both are one game client on our own engine, qwzspace: 3D first, then 2D, then text as fallbacks").
- **The vision: a wuxian MMORPG that is real business unfolding.** The board's enterprises are temples and courts. Agents doing routines are seen going to work. "if you look we almost fucking have the temples and courts, you got us cloooose to the vision of a wuxian MMORPG that's actually real world business unfolding, agents doing routines are really going to work".
- **Users and agents both inhabit it.** The MUD experience is for agents as much as for humans.

## The motifs the Leader named

Each motif is a world mechanic. Each is also a mathematical object the house already holds.
- **Coloured Wang tiles and edges.** World tiling whose matching rules are visible. In theory: the Wang–Burgers correspondence, Berger's aperiodicity read as forced translational holonomy (2026-07-15 conjectures on infometrologia).
- **The four-colour J26 gyrobifastigium exchange cells.** The board's exchange cells (2026-09-03 note on the 561 Group as a board).
- **Burgers-vector dislocations.** Defects in the tiling that show where work does not close, as H¹ obstructions.
- **Holofoam presence.** Signed-in players and agents appear in the foam.
- **Occupy White Walls–style galleries.** Player- and agent-curated rooms of work. The Leader listed OWW among the interfaces MetroPlat should be like. The VaultMaze note of 2026-09-27 carries OWW galleries for VaultMaze.
