## Purpose and selected carrier

This LLD selects H-02's carrier and proposes its evidence correspondence for R-04 and R-09. The source-event mapping, lossy readout laws, and operational realization still require proof and assay. A person's current usable context is a scoped query over attributable observation and disposition events. It is not a flat memory string, latest-value register, confidence score, proof object, permission, execution outcome, or receiver acceptance.

The selected value carrier is science's completed record `ORecord = Option Nat × Option Nat` from `Obsmetrologia.Language.Positing.Record`: the positive and negative coordinates are tallies, `some n` is a finite tally, and `none` denotes the limit value ω. Its knowledge order increases both tallies; its truth order increases positive and decreases negative support. The module proves the interlaced bilattice laws (`oRecordInterlaced`) and proves that FOUR embeds injectively into ORecord (`four_embeds`). This is a map FOUR → ORecord, not a map ORecord → FOUR and not an inclusion of finite tally values: the four embedded readings map to ORecord's four bounds, whose non-bottom coordinates are ω. H-02 MUST retain ORecord and attributable support/opposition records; a proposed lossy ORecord → FOUR readout below MUST NOT stand in for the carrier.

The LLD selects the ω-bounded transfinite regime actually defined in that science module. It does not claim arbitrary ordinal-valued support or the still-unrealized non-idempotent, aperiodic stage-closure carrier described in `TransfiniteUpToClosure.lean`. This selection treats the demand's transfinite requirement as requiring at least a formal limit beyond every finite evidence stage; if later customer clarification requires stages beyond ω, that is a changed carrier requirement, not a reason to relabel FOUR.

## Evidence facts and identity

Each retained observation is an immutable, attributable journal fact with: stable fact identity and canonical content digest; claim/subject identity; polarity (`supports` or `opposes`); source principal and source reference; acquisition method; observed time and source revision where known; declared use/disclosure scope; freshness evidence or explicit absence; causal dependencies; and the admitted context/owner. The owning journal admission and authenticated context contract provide identity, authorization, retention, and disclosure enforcement. A body-supplied source name, timestamp, scope, or authority verdict does not authenticate itself.

A causal dependency identifies evidence on which a derived observation relies. Derived claims keep their own identity and dependencies and MUST NOT be counted as independent source corroboration merely because they have a different model/request/event id. The query returns only dependency/source references the principal is authorized to inspect. Unauthorized dependencies are not read and their identity, existence, edges, and contribution to any returned count are not disclosed. H-02 does not infer source independence, truth probability, reliability, or a confidence weight from tally size.

Retrying an identical admitted evidence fact under its stable identity and digest is idempotent and contributes once. Reuse of that identity with changed bytes is refused. A genuinely new observation is retained as a new occurrence, even if its claim and polarity match earlier evidence; the returned tally is occurrence count, not strength or truth rank. Its source and dependencies remain visible to authorized readers so repeated reports cannot masquerade as independent evidence. This applies existing L-I02 replay identity discipline to evidence admission; it does not assume a new transport protocol.

## Carrier correspondence and laws

For one claim and one query projection, form separate support and opposition collections from the distinct eligible observation identities. The ORecord coordinate is the tally of admitted occurrences in that polarity for the projection: finite cardinality `n` is `some n`; `none` is permitted only when the evidence construction supplies a witness that the countably staged occurrence process is unbounded and identifies `none` as its least upper bound. A counter overflow, cap, model assertion, elapsed time, or a finite collection is not such a witness. No arbitrary ordinal rank or numeric confidence is introduced.

On appending one new eligible support (respectively opposition) occurrence, the corresponding finite tally increments by one and the other coordinate is preserved; exact duplicates do not increment. For an operational finite journal frontier, a complete query returns the complete authorized eligible set when within the admitted response bound; otherwise it returns a finite page and a continuation bound to the same authorized frontier, with completeness stated explicitly. It MUST NOT claim that an infinite set has been enumerated. Combining snapshots MUST deduplicate stable occurrence identities before tally construction; the ORecord lattice join itself is max, not multiset addition, and is not a substitute for evidence-event union. The truth and knowledge lattice operations have exactly their science-defined meanings; the implementation correspondence must assay their laws rather than invent alternative aggregation.

The ω-limit law is the science-defined `settles_at_omega`: each finite stage is below the ω value, no finite stage reaches it, and ω is the least upper bound/fixed point for the `osuccPos` increment dynamic. This is a stage-limit theorem, not an operational assertion that a finite runtime has accumulated infinitely many observations. Current operational query semantics are finite-frontier only and therefore return finite tallies; the `none = ω` value is reserved to a mathematical unbounded-chain construction with its LUB witness. No operational lazy/infinite enumeration protocol is specified here. Until such a protocol and its witness/access contract are separately specified and proved, an operational result MUST NOT emit ω or imply that all members of an unbounded set have been inspected. Counter overflow, caps, model assertions, or elapsed time never establish ω.

The science theorem `four_embeds` proves only the embedding `boundsMap : Reading → ORecord` and maps FOUR's four bounds to ORecord's four bound values. It does not prove a reverse readout. The LLD proposes a separate lossy readout `readout : ORecord → Reading`, defined as `Reading.ofSlots (positive tally > 0) (negative tally > 0)`, where `none` counts as > 0 and `some 0` does not. Thus `(some 0, some 0)` reads neither, `(some n, some 0)` for n > 0 reads support only, `(some 0, some n)` for n > 0 reads opposition only, and any pair with both coordinates positive (including ω) reads both. This mapping's order/lattice laws and its left-inverse law over `boundsMap` are new correspondence proof obligations; `four_embeds` proves none of them. Any display using `readout` MUST be labeled lossy and omit no underlying ORecord/source access path. It discards tally multiplicity, provenance, dependencies, and ω information. An absence of admitted evidence means no evidence in this query/scope, not evidence for the contrary.

## Query eligibility, conflict, freshness, and correction

An H-02 query is bound to principal, owner/context, intended use, subject/claim selectors, required source/freshness conditions, and current policy. After authorization, it returns the eligible support/opposition evidence accessible to that principal, the ORecord tally for the same authorized projection, inspectable dependency/source references, freshness status, and applicable correction/disposition chain only to the extent the caller may read each referenced fact. It MUST preserve simultaneous support and opposition. A conflict does not select the newest row, disappear, imply arbitrariness, or authorize an action. Exclusion reporting is limited to evidence metadata the principal is independently authorized to read. For a denied context/source, H-02 MUST NOT disclose content, identity, existence, dependencies, counts, or revealing exclusion reasons; it returns only a non-disclosing refusal. No global inventory of unauthorized or private evidence is implied.

Freshness is a query policy over observed times, source revisions, or other admitted freshness evidence. No universal TTL, decay curve, or silent freshness threshold is implied. If the caller requires freshness and the source cannot establish it under the declared policy, return `unknown/stale/insufficient` with the source evidence; do not silently promote the observation to current.

Correction, source withdrawal, and use restriction are new attributable facts referring to exact prior fact identities. They never rewrite or erase the original observation. A correction supplies replacement claim evidence only when it actually contains such a claim; otherwise it only records a correction/disposition. A user with authority over personal context may restrict future use of that context under the admitted policy, but cannot rewrite a third-party source's original assertion. A source correction is attributable to that source only when independently authenticated as such. Current query eligibility follows the applicable authority and policy chain; a withdrawn, superseded, expired, or out-of-scope fact remains in the causal record but is excluded from that query projection with its reason. A correction may therefore change a later authorized query's tally without retracting prior history or previously observed effects. A caller may inspect prior facts/dependencies only while separately authorized to read them; correction history MUST NOT be used to bypass source/context access controls. Retention obligations and authorized deletion/disposition remain those of the owning journal contract; this LLD creates no erasure exception or indefinite-retention grant.

## Knowledge, proof, authority, and receiving effects

ORecord is an evidence-value carrier, not a proof calculus. An observed source claim, model derivation, theorem/proof object, human correction, permission decision, provider acknowledgement, and independently observed receiver state are distinct fact kinds with distinct issuers and validation rules. ORecord support or opposition alone never establishes a theorem, factual truth, authority, consent, successful execution, or acceptance. Formal proof requires a separately typed derivation with checkable premises/rule correspondence; H-02 does not label an observation as proof.

H-03 may use the returned evidence to analyze and propose work. The live operation boundary re-resolves current authority, scope, preconditions, and effect identity independently; a support tally cannot grant permission. H-04's attempt/provider result is retained as another observation. The actual receiver is queried under its own authority, and any required receiving predicate and authenticated human acceptance remain separate from provider acknowledgement and platform settlement (S-05, H-I4, L-I06/L-I07). Reconciliation after an uncertain effect MUST inspect that receiver evidence before retry; evidence accumulation must not itself dispatch or repeat an effect.

These contracts preserve the MAPE-K transformation across the shared H-02/H-03/H-04 boundary: Monitor admits evidence, Analyze returns the evidence and uncertainty, Plan proposes an admissible next step, Execute acts only through H-04, and Knowledge retains resulting observations and derivations. The acronym creates no separate service or authorization path.

## Required independent observations

Before claiming H-02 conformity, an independent assay must demonstrate:

1. Distinct finite support and opposition facts yield the expected ORecord tallies, retain all authorized source/dependency references, and permit both coordinates to be nonzero; unauthorized references contribute no leaked metadata; the finite FOUR view is marked lossy.
2. An identical replay contributes once, changed bytes under an existing identity refuse, and a genuinely new repeated report increments occurrence count without being described as stronger or independent evidence.
3. Conflicting sources remain concurrently queryable; correction adds a new attributable event and changes only the applicable later projection; old source facts and dependencies remain inspectable only to principals authorized to read them.
4. Source withdrawal, user use restriction, changed freshness, and changed purpose affect query eligibility only under their admitted authority/policy. They return explicit exclusion reasons only when those metadata are authorized for the querying principal; otherwise the query refuses without revealing the source or its count. These conditions do not create a truth verdict or erase historical outcomes.
5. A finite event stream cannot produce ω by counter saturation or model assertion. A separately constructed unbounded chain satisfies the exact least-upper-bound correspondence at ω; absent that construction, operational query returns finite values only.
6. A model proposal or supporting tally cannot authorize an action; a formal proof claim requires its checker/evidence; provider success cannot satisfy a receiver predicate. After an uncertain external attempt, receiver reconciliation precedes any retry.
7. Personal, executive, and delegated-work queries enforce owner, purpose, disclosure, and source scope before reading or exposing evidence. Verify that a forbidden source/context is not read and that content, identity, existence, dependencies, counts, and identifying exclusion reasons do not leak through a query, projection, correction chain, or refusal.

## Realization boundary and open obligations

This LLD selects the science-defined carrier and proposes an abstract event-to-tally correspondence; it is a design contract, not evidence of running behavior. H-O1 remains open for proof of the ORecord/event mapping, reverse readout laws, a permitted ω-limit witness/access interface, and runtime conformance. The carrier choice is narrowed to the ω-bounded regime, but the full correspondence is not yet closed. No Muse Assist code is yet claimed to implement ORecord, its law-preserving update, source attribution, correction projection, or ω-limit witness. Downstream fabrication must bind journal event identities and source references to the abstract facts above, define the concrete serialization and exact finite update, and provide a separately controlled assay against these laws before runtime claims.

The journal remains authoritative for admission, causal identity, access, retention, and disposition. This LLD does not add a second database, universal evidence service, independent task store, numerical scoring model, automatic conflict resolution, or write authority. Existing code or schemas are reusable only after their mapping preserves these properties. The operative query must return enough authorized source evidence to be useful in personal context, executive responsibility, research, planning, and post-effect receiving assessment; a technical evidence index with no use in those work paths does not realize H-02. It MUST NOT enumerate or expose evidence outside the querying principal's current read/use/disclosure permissions.
