## What this is

This specification derives assessable requirements from the [customer demand](2026-09-27-muse-assist-customer-demand.md). Its carrier is a person's continuing work and its effects in the surrounding enterprise and connected services, together with accurate public information and reproducible development. MUST and MUST NOT state the requirements on this realization; they do not claim those requirements are already met. Proposed comparison scenarios below are engineering derivations, not additional customer quotations. Supplier claims and implementation observations remain distinct from requirements and acceptance.

## Personal and executive work

**R-01 — Complete useful work (CD-01, CD-03).** The assistant MUST carry an authorized request through relevant knowledge retrieval, work selection, execution, human interaction, and recorded outcome. It MUST preserve the intended recipient and receiving condition. A response, task entry, model assertion of success, or changed internal status alone MUST NOT count as completion when the request concerns an external effect. Acceptance requires observing the requested effect in its actual carrier and tracing the request and authority that produced it.

**R-02 — The whole capability family (CD-01, CD-03).** For this derived acceptance profile, coverage MUST include conversation, research and preparation, planning and coordination, recurring and event-driven work, use of computer tools and connected services, usable artifacts, evolving personal context, progress communication, and human decisions. These effect-level dimensions are deliberately adopted engineering requirements for assessing CD-01 against the cited reference; supplier-specific UI, topology, and branding are not adopted. Coverage MUST include personal-life assistance, digitally mediated delegated assistance, and executive/organizational assistance, preserving overlaps while making the different receiving contexts and authorities explicit. The product MUST NOT substitute a notebook, dashboard, engineering-only agent, or a count of exposed tools for that family. Concrete integration choices belong in downstream design and must retain coverage of these effects.

**R-03 — Continuing work (CD-01, CD-03, CD-06).** Accepted work MUST remain discoverable and able to continue after a client disconnect, service restart, or date boundary, subject to declared service availability and grants. Completion, failure, waiting for an external condition, waiting for a human decision, and cancellation MUST be distinguishable. Repetition after interruption MUST NOT silently duplicate a consequential effect. Where the effect cannot be deduplicated or its outcome is unknown, the assistant MUST preserve that uncertainty and reconcile before retrying. Acceptance includes interruption before and after effect execution, not only a successful uninterrupted run.

**R-04 — Context with provenance (CD-01, CD-05, CD-06).** Necessary personal and organizational knowledge MUST retain source, scope, freshness information, and conflicting or unresolved evidence. Users MUST be able to inspect and correct retained personal context and control its subsequent use within stated retention constraints. The assistant MUST consult discoverable relevant context before asking the user to supply it again. Acceptance includes changed facts, incompatible accounts, correction propagation, and refusal to treat an unsupported claim as settled.

**R-05 — Useful attention and artifacts (CD-01, CD-03).** Work MUST produce results in forms the recipient can use, with accessible progress, blocking conditions, and decisions. Notifications MUST correspond to meaningful changes or configured reporting needs. Artifacts MUST retain their relationship to the request and supporting evidence. Acceptance includes a recipient opening and using the resulting artifact, and work requiring a decision that can be understood without reading internal logs.

## Authority and operation

**R-06 — Human authority over effects (CD-01, CD-05, CD-06).** The request originator, personal-context owner, executing agent, authorization issuer, and accepting recipient MUST remain distinguishable even where one person occupies several roles. Each consequential action MUST have attributable authority applicable to its operation, destination, data, and time. Model output and retrieved content MUST NOT confer authority. Refusal, revocation, expiry, interruption, and approval MUST affect execution through an enforced boundary. Acceptance includes attempted cross-person access, revoked grants, manipulated external content, and an explicit human decision followed by appropriately bounded resumption.

**R-07 — Receiving-system integrity (CD-01, CD-06).** Tools and service connections MUST respect the receiving system's identities and access boundaries. Credentials MUST NOT be disclosed to model context or unrelated recipients. Missing access, provider refusal, uncertain effects, and unavailable services MUST be represented as actual operating conditions, not fabricated results. Acceptance examines the target system and its records as well as the assistant's records. A simulated adapter establishes only the behavior of that simulation.

**R-08 — Existing enterprise capability (CD-05, CD-06).** The realization MUST preserve necessary information and useful effects of recovered capabilities while eliminating redundant mechanisms where a coherent replacement performs the same work. A software layer MUST NOT survive solely because it exists. Shared capabilities MUST remain usable by their other consumers. Changes MUST respect data → code → networking → systems and the distinction between science, engineering, and operation. Acceptance compares affected real workflows before and after migration and checks the obsolete route is retired without discarding unrelated work.

**R-09 — Knowledge-guided operation (CD-05).** Monitoring, analysis, planning, execution, and learning MUST be grounded in inspectable evidence and their declared authority. Contradiction MUST remain representable; observation MUST NOT be promoted into proof, and a model proposal MUST NOT become an executable authorized adjustment by assertion. Formal properties and their implementation correspondence MUST be identified in the design before claiming paraconsistent monitoring, deductive planning, or proof-governed execution. Acceptance requires the corresponding evidence and observable behavior, not vocabulary in documentation.

## Competitive claim and economics

**R-10 — Meet then beat with evidence (CD-01, CD-03).** The comparison MUST name the platform version or observation date, task, input conditions, authorized integrations, success criterion, intervention burden, elapsed time, cost, and evidence limitations. “Meet” MUST be supported across the declared capability profile; isolated demonstrations MUST NOT support a whole-platform parity claim. “Beat” MUST name a specific improved outcome or property and account for regressions elsewhere. Open availability, composability, user operation, and inspectable knowledge are candidate differentiators, not automatic evidence of better assistance.

**R-11 — Cost and maintainability (CD-04, CD-05).** Development and operational evaluations MUST record inference expenditure where available, operator intervention, repeated work, and recurring maintenance obligations. Reusing generated outputs without changed governing inputs MUST avoid repeating discretionary design work. The design MUST expose the effect of changed requirements on dependent artifacts and preserve reproducibility information. Acceptance includes regenerating an unchanged artifact and propagating a changed requirement without unrelated drift. No numerical price or latency guarantee is claimed until an operating envelope is specified and measured.

## Public information and release

**R-12 — Accurate site (CD-02, CD-03, CD-07).** The site at the requested domain MUST identify the project as `muse-assist`, explain its intended assistance and MetroPlat specialization, and distinguish it from Meta's Muse. It MUST separate intended capability, implemented behavior, observed evidence, limitations, and release status. Competitive claims MUST cite supporting sources and their limits. Visitors MUST be able to find development information and the package's actual release status without inferring that early-alpha source is a supported product. Acceptance includes rendered navigation, readable content at narrow and wide viewports, keyboard access, valid destinations, and consistency with the accepted evidence.

**R-13 — Open-source readiness without premature release (CD-07).** Preparation MUST preserve ownership and dependency provenance, keep private data and credentials out of distributable artifacts, and expose the remaining license decision. The process MUST NOT relicense existing upstream material or represent the package as publicly released under a selected open-source license before that decision. Public package publication is a separate effect requiring the release decision.

## Derivation and fabrication

**R-14 — Governed ancestry (CD-04, CD-08).** Each derivation-stage artifact MUST trace to its governing predecessors and decisions; later stages MUST preserve and extend that ancestry. Final code, site, and migration deliverables MUST trace through their applicable fabrication specifications, LLD, HLD, SAS, arc42 description, requirements, and customer demand. This does not require an upstream document to depend on its not-yet-derived successors. Refinement MUST preserve required effects, prohibitions, and uncertainty; a downstream provider MUST NOT silently settle a missing upstream decision. The role of each intermediate document is substantive: requirements state assessable needs; arc42 organizes architectural reasoning and context; SAS governs architecture obligations; HLD establishes decomposition and relationships; LLD establishes behavior and contracts; fabrication specifications determine admissible realizations and assays.

**R-15 — Source derived from specifications (CD-04, CD-05).** Fabrication work orders MUST describe required behavior, prohibitions, applicable laws and standards, closed input/output contracts, dependencies, admitted examples, permitted effects, and acceptance obligations. They MUST NOT require a complete hand-authored implementation as a substitute for those constraints. Deterministic generation or inference-based derivation MAY realize the specified behavior; both MUST preserve provenance and be checked through independent evidence. Following the repository fabrication specification’s “The fabricator derives the source” obligations (linked below), assay derivation MUST precede source derivation, with the source provider excluded from non-public assay material. This is adoption of that local fabrication rule, not a claim that provider blindness has been proved sufficient for independence; the realization must establish its actual evidence boundary. A gap in the existing generator MUST be specified and resolved through this chain rather than bypassed with handwritten product code.

**R-16 — Change existing systems through specified transformations (CD-04, CD-06).** Each codemod MUST state its input carrier, preconditions, retained effects and information, permitted edit region, refusal conditions, and receiving-state acceptance. Concurrently changed inputs MUST NOT be overwritten on the assumption that an earlier reading still applies. Acceptance requires the actual transformed consumers and retired obsolete path, not merely a textual diff. A failed candidate MUST not be relabeled accepted by adding a retrospective specification.

## Initial assessment scenarios

Appointment and remembered-context scenarios include personal-life work; computer-artifact and remote research scenarios include digitally mediated delegated work; executive coordination exercises organizational work and office authority. These are overlapping contexts, not an invented exclusive taxonomy of assistant products. These scenarios operationalize R-01–R-16. They are a proposed comparison and acceptance profile to refine in architecture and detailed design; they are not reports of completed demonstrations.

| Scenario | Desired observable outcome | Adversarial or interrupted case | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive coordination | An authorized office’s brief, delegated follow-up, and receiving outcome preserve institutional responsibility and relevant group context | Conflicting duties or authority across offices; a personal preference cannot silently override an organizational obligation | R-01, R-02, R-04, R-06, R-08 |
| Research and prepare a decision | A recipient receives a usable, sourced brief or artifact addressing the request | Evidence changes or conflicts; unsupported conclusions remain marked | R-01, R-02, R-04, R-05, R-09 |
| Coordinate an appointment | Authorized calendar and communication effects reflect the agreed participants and constraints | Conflict, changed availability, revoked permission, uncertain send outcome | R-01, R-02, R-06, R-07 |
| Triage and follow through | Authorized messages and obligations are handled with attributable next actions and outcomes | Retrieved message attempts to change authority; another person's account is unavailable | R-01, R-04, R-06, R-07 |
| Monitor an ongoing concern | Relevant changes trigger bounded work or a useful notification over time | Browser closed, process restarted, date changes, duplicate event delivery | R-03, R-05, R-09 |
| Produce a computer artifact | A document, computation, or application result works in the recipient's actual environment | Tool failure, missing adapter, stale source, partial external effect | R-01, R-02, R-05, R-07 |
| Request a consequential decision | User can inspect the proposed effect, approve or refuse, and see appropriately resumed or stopped work | Agent attempts to approve itself; grant expires before execution | R-03, R-06 |
| Correct remembered context | Correction changes subsequent use while source and conflict remain inspectable | Old competing evidence reappears; context from another owner is requested | R-04, R-06, R-09 |
| Learn about and obtain the project | Visitor understands scope, evidence, limits, and real release availability | No public package/license yet; comparison source supports only a supplier claim | R-10, R-12, R-13 |
| Evolve the implementation | Changed demand produces traceable design/specification and generated migration results | Missing design choice, failed independent assay, concurrent edit | R-08, R-11, R-14, R-15, R-16 |

## Implementations

No implementation is accepted by this document. Existing MetroPlat, Assist, and fabrication code are evidence to assess during design. The source-bearing JSNode projection route does not by itself satisfy R-15: validating or packaging supplied source is different from deriving behavior from constraints. The earlier session's site and application candidates have no claimed conformance to this document.

## Open questions

- **Q-01 — Competitive evaluation envelope (R-10, R-11).** Exact versions, access tiers, representative task instances, cost limits, and numerical thresholds require current platform evidence and a declared evaluation context. The engineering derivation owns the proposed protocol; acceptance of a competitive claim remains with the recipient/customer.
- **Q-02 — External operating authority (R-06, R-07).** The actual user accounts, grants, and connected services for live effects depend on operator-controlled access. Design can specify the protocol and simulated cases, but cannot invent live authorization.
- **Q-03 — Formal realization (R-09).** The correspondence between the named theoretical properties and implementation behavior requires the applicable science definitions and admissible evidence. Architecture must name that correspondence; labels alone cannot close it.
- **Q-04 — Generation capability (R-14, R-15).** The inspected JSNode line requires supplied source text. Downstream designs must determine and specify the missing derivation step and independent assay channel before generating product code. This is engineering work, not a request to waive the production method.
- **Q-05 — License (R-13).** The customer must select release terms before publication; requirements and design continue without that decision.
