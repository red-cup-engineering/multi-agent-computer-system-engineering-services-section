01 / THE CAPABILITY FAMILY

Hand off the work.
Stay in the loop.

The product is the continuing ability to get things done on a person’s behalf. These six connected capabilities define the scope of development.

01 / CONTINUING WORK

Goals that survive a chat.

Turn an intention into work, continue across sessions, respond to events and schedules, and recover when a step fails or needs your decision.

02 / AN EXECUTION ENVIRONMENT

Tools that do real work.

Use files, code, a browser, and connected services to research, prepare, organize, and act. Produce usable documents, applications, and other artifacts.

03 / CONNECTED LIFE

Across apps and people.

Bring communication, calendars, relationships, and everyday administration into the work. Keep each service’s actual authority and access boundaries intact.

04 / DURABLE KNOWLEDGE

Context that develops.

Retain relevant knowledge with its sources, uncertainty, and scope. Let people inspect and correct it. Learn from results without mistaking a claim for an outcome.

05 / MEANINGFUL ATTENTION

Proactive, with purpose.

Watch for changes that affect the goal. Surface useful findings or required decisions while routine work continues, without demanding constant supervision.

06 / PERSONAL AUTHORITY

Control over the effects.

Make grants, refusals, interruptions, and approvals part of execution. Keep credentials and permission enforcement outside the model’s discretion.

Product requirements, not a claim that every capability is already implemented or deployed.

02 / THE STANDARD TO MEET

Meta’s Muse is
a concrete reference.

Muse is Meta’s product. muse-assist is this project. The comparison starts with the full personal-agent experience, not the shared word in the name.

01 Work across tools and time +

Meta describes a personal computer with a browser and development tools, concurrent goals, event- and schedule-driven activity, editable memory, and rich artifacts. The corresponding bar for muse-assist is a goal carried through actual execution, including after the client disconnects.

Read Meta’s product design →
02 Enforced control, beyond a prompt +

Meta describes an isolated runtime, separate credential handling, and a Sentinel that governs actions and network access. Its permission decisions are enforced outside the agent. muse-assist must demonstrate its own authority boundaries; a log or an approval message alone cannot establish equivalent protection.

Read Meta’s technical account →
03 An everyday personal assistant +

The reference experience spans planning, communication, research, shopping, and administration through connected apps and a browser. muse-assist’s success must be evaluated on real end-to-end tasks: information found, work performed, decisions respected, and outcomes confirmed.

Read Meta’s launch account →

These are Meta’s published descriptions, not our independent performance measurements. Its Confidential VM was announced as future work; we do not treat it as an available launch capability.

03 / THE METROPLAT REALIZATION

Build from the
underlying capabilities.

The 561 Group has substantial prior work in agentic systems, knowledge, execution, and services. That material is early alpha. Existing code is evidence to recover and improve, not proof of an operating product.

SPECIALIZED METROPLAT SERVER

Personal work on a shared foundation.

muse-assist brings the personal-agent experience together at the application boundary. Shared knowledge, work selection, execution, communication, and recorded outcomes should remain usable throughout the ecosystem.

Inspect the development boundary ↗

Meet the practical baseline

Connect conversation to real tools, durable work, human decisions, and results. Verify the complete path rather than counting interfaces or advertised features.

Then go further

Pursue inspectable knowledge, explicit treatment of conflicting evidence, portable authority, and cooperation across independently operated systems.

Prove the difference

Open-source intent and architectural theory do not establish superior assistance. Reliability, useful outcomes, and retained human control must be demonstrated.

Release status and wider comparison

muse-assist is in development. There is no published open-source package or hosted assistant service yet, and release licensing remains undecided. No feature-parity or superiority claim has been established.

Meta’s Muse is a central reference, alongside the real capabilities of other platforms such as Microsoft Copilot Cowork and Gemini in Google Workspace. Comparative work must account for access, permissions, failures, and results—not just product descriptions.
