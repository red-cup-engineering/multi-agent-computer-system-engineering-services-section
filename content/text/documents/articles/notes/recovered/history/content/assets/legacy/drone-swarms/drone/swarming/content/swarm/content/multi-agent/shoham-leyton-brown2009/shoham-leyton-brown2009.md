# Shoham & Leyton-Brown 2009 — Multiagent Systems

Shoham, Y., & Leyton-Brown, K.
(2009). *Multiagent Systems: Algorithmic, Game-Theoretic, and Logical Foundations*.
Cambridge University Press.

The standard graduate textbook unifying game theory, mechanism design, computational social choice, distributed AI, and logical foundations of multi-agent systems.
Notable for its breadth: covers normal-form and extensive-form games, equilibrium concepts and refinements, auction theory, mechanism design, social choice, coalition formation, communication languages, multi-agent planning, learning, and logics for multi- agent systems.

The book defines the contemporary scope of multi-agent systems as a unified field bridging economics, computer science, and AI.
Its chapter organization has become standard for the field; many subsequent textbooks and courses follow its structure.

Distinctive features:

- Algorithmic emphasis throughout — not just existence results but computability and complexity.
- Integration of distributed AI heritage with mainstream game theory and mechanism design.
- Substantial coverage of computational social choice (a relatively new area at time of writing).
- Available open-access in PDF form, lowering barriers to use.
